## Phase 4 — Expand from real lifecycle integrations

- **4.1 B38/B43** `[DONE]` — The scheduler tick now runs under a cross-process, non-blocking advisory lock (`_tick_lock`: `fcntl.flock` on POSIX, `msvcrt.locking` on Windows) held on an open descriptor so the OS releases it on process death — a crash never leaves a stale lock. A tick that cannot acquire the lock is skipped (returns `[]`), giving non-overlap between concurrent scheduler processes/daemon invocations. Due jobs are claimed durably *before* execution: `next_run` is advanced to the next occurrence after the tick time and persisted before `submit_and_run`, so a crash mid-run cannot refire the same occurrence; each outcome is persisted immediately after its job.
