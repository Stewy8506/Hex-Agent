# Plan 18.01: Experience Library Integration into HarnessOrchestrator

<objective>
Wire Google Research Harness²'s per-domain Experience Library (`harness2.core.library.Library`) directly into `HarnessOrchestrator`. Persist structured post-task lessons (cue, lesson, evidence, and adopted tree) under `.hex/harness2_library/` (or configurable data directory), rebuild `INDEX.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`, and inject retrieved domain experience into agent planning and autonomous repair prompts.

Purpose: Enables cross-task experiential learning where verified solutions and failure mitigations automatically inform future tasks in the same domain, preventing recurring regressions.
Output: Integrated `Library` engine in `src/harness_core/orchestrator.py` with comprehensive unit and integration tests in `tests/test_harness2_library_wiring.py`.
</objective>
